Your avatar can have a variety of companions, called Pets. These Pets show up on the map and are seen by other players beside your avatar. They give no advantages and are merely cosmetic. If you are hanged by the town, some pets will cry next to your body (including Doge, My Little Friend, Cerberus and others). Some pets will also have a unique taunt animation.

The Kickstarter Fairy pet was obtainable by pledging $250 or more to any one of four Kickstarters by BlankMediaGames.

The Green Dragon pet was obtainable by pledging $25 or more to BlankMediaGames' first Kickstarter which ended on March 21st of 2014.

The Mini-Dlive and Mini-Entoan pets were available by pledging $25 or more to BlankMediaGames' second Kickstarter which ended on October 18th of 2014.

The Mini-LordMinion777, Mini-OhmWrecker, Mini-RPGMinx, and Piggy Bank pets were obtainable by pledging $50 or more to BlankMediaGames' second Kickstarter.

The Sir Hamelot pet was obtainable by pledging $35 or more to BlankMediaGames' third Kickstarter which ended on May 26th of 2016.

The Miss Hamelot pet was obtainable by pledging $25 or more to BlankMediaGames' fourth Kickstarter which ended on December 22nd of 2017.

Special Pets[]

These are the pets which may be obtained by completing a specific task.

Polly

Friend Not Food

Webby

Golden Doge

Pestilence Horse

Phone-ix

Polly may be purchased as part of a bundle, one of which can always be found in the game's shop.

Friend Not Food may be obtained by referring 3 friends.

Webby may be obtained by purchasing Web Premium, which can be found under the "Account" tab in the game's shop while playing on the browser version.

Golden Doge was a reward for players who achieved a season high of 1400+ Elo in Ranked Season Three.

Pestilence Horse was a reward for players who achieved a season high of 1400+ Elo in Ranked Season Five.

Phone-ix may be obtained by purchasing Mobile Premium, which can be found under the "Account" tab in the game's shop while playing on the mobile version.

Trivia[]

XJ-0462 is most likely a reference to the Ratchet and Clank series, as Clank is designated XJ-0461.

I Hate You All is a reference to the real life cat Tardar Sauce, known on the internet as Grumpy Cat due to her face having looked like she was constantly grumpy.

As of now, it takes exactly 44,720 Town Points to purchase every single Pet without Merit Points. It sums up to be exactly $172 USD based on the currency's root value (260 Town Points equals $1 USD).
