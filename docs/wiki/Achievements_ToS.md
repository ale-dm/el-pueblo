# Achievements (ToS)

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_(ToS)

Achievements are awards you earn when you perform a certain task in the game. Some are much easier to obtain than others.
As of Version 1.5.9, achievements reward Merit Points instead of Town Points when completed and each achievement may only be obtained once. Below is the list of all achievements. See this page for a guide on obtaining some of the achievements.

The screen a player receives when they get an achievement. In this case, the player was a Necromancer who reanimated Pestilence (which will cause their death unless healed)

If you are promoted as a member of the Mafia, become a Vigilante as a Vampire Hunter, remember a role as an Amnesiac, or become Pestilence, winning will only count for your original role. If you turn into a Jester as an Executioner, turn into a Survivor as a Guardian Angel whose target has died (at Night for Executioner), or are converted into a Vampire, winning will only count for your new role.
