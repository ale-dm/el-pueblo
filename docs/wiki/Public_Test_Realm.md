Trivia

The PTR was opened to the public in 2015 to test out the new-at-the-time roles Vampire Hunter and Vampire.

The PTR was opened to the public in June of 2017 for players to test out the new-at-the-time Coven Expansion. After its release, players were prompted to buy the DLC on the regular site.

In May of 2020, the PTR was opened to test the new-at-the-time Town Traitor game modes.
