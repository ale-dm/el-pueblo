Taunts were introduced in Version 1.5.11. Taunts allow players to anonymously cause an animation to appear to playfully tease or as by their name, taunt a target.

Taunts may be purchased in the shop for 50 Town Points, 200 Merit Points, in a special offer (paid with real money), or gained as a reward from the Daily Brew in batches of 10.

A player may own up to 100 of a single taunt.

Taunts do not need to be equipped. They are automatically equipped.

Taunts are a one-time use, which lasts for the remainder of the phase.

If a player who was taunted is voted to go on trial, the taunt's effects on that player immediately vanish.

Taunts can be seen by everyone, aside from when a dead player uses the taunt; then only dead players may see the taunt. This is to prevent the dead from making some form of communication to the living, and also to stop living players anonymously framing others. 

Clicking on a player's avatar or on a player's name in the list of players will open up a menu of available taunts that may be delivered.

Taunts can be performed during the Discussion phase and during the Night phase. It is prohibited in every other phase. 

Taunts can be used on any player. As such, players that are alive can use Taunts on themselves, dead players can use Taunts on other players, and Jailed players can use Taunts on others. The only restriction on using a Taunt is if a player has left the game.

A player may only be taunted once per Day and once per Night, however, the same player can taunt multiple players at a time.

Many players use taunts to call targets at nighttime or to trick people into thinking someone may be attacked.

RIP - A portal appears. A skeleton hand then comes out of that portal. It goes back in, and comes out again with a RIP sign, and places it in front of the targeted player.

Accursed - A witch appears, and turns the targeted player into a frog, then vanishes. If the targeted player is dead, a dead frog appears.

Defiled - A doge appears, urinates on the targeted player, then vanishes.

Celebrate - Fireworks explode above the targeted player.

Winter Wonder - Snow falls around the targeted player.

Sucked Dry - Bats appear around the targeted player.
