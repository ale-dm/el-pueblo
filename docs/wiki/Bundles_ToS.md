# Bundles (ToS)

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Bundles_(ToS)

In Version 1.2.1, BlankMediaGames introduced promotional bundles. Random bundles appear once per day when the player is logged into the Town of Salem game and these deals may be purchased by using real-life currency.

These offers will be at a random discount up to 80% off and they are available for 5 or 10 minutes. If a person owns one or more cosmetics which are found in a specific bundle, that bundle will not ever appear as a person cannot purchase duplicates.

The player is immediately granted his or her rewards after purchase. If a player has purchased a bundle and has not received their rewards, they may send an email to support@townofsalem.io with their receipt and their username.
