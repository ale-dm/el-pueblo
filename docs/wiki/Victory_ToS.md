# Victory (ToS)

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_(ToS)

A victory, or win, is the accomplishment of a player's goal, as shown on their role card.

Most commonly, a victory is achieved for a faction ( Town, Mafia, Coven, all Neutral Killing and 2 Neutral Chaos roles) once there are no living members of the other factions (killed, hanged or converted). Some Neutral Benign, all Neutral Evils and 1 Neutral Chaos ( Pirate) role may win independently of any faction because they have different goals.

A win may occur at the beginning of the Day (after deaths are publicly announced) or after a successful hang. If all remaining players die at the same time, it is a draw by wipeout.

When members of opposing factions suicide (leave the game), they are automatically counted as dead (this means that the number of votes required changes accordingly during the Voting phase, and most roles are unable to use their ability on said players). However, a win will only trigger once their deaths are announced ( Night death or hang). If a player is hanged while all other opposing players left the game, the game will automatically end after the Last Words phase, even if their death was not publicly announced. This means that the game may end unexpectedly after hanging a Townie.

If there are no deaths in two consecutive Days late in the game, a draw by timeout will occur despite opposing factions being left alive. The earliest a draw by timeout can occur is Day 9.

It is possible to have games without any opposing faction. However, this is only possible in Custom/Rapid Mode, All Any, and Coven All Any. When this happens, the game will immediately end as soon as Day 2 starts. It is impossible to end a game Day 1.

Usually, the Host will be unable to start a Custom/Rapid Mode game without an opposing faction. However, this requirement is automatically considered satisfied if there is a Witch so, for example, a game can start with 2 Witches, a Survivor, and 4 Serial Killers, 4 Mafia members, or 4 Vampires. This setup is popular in winfarms.

A kingmaker occurs when two opposite factions are left alive with one non-faction role, such as a Survivor or Executioner. One simple occurrence would be a Godfather and a Serial Killer left alive with a Survivor. The game will not end until a draw by timeout occurs, the Survivor votes to hang either the Godfather or Serial Killer, or the Survivor gets killed at Night if they don't have enough vests (most likely by the Serial Killer, so that they win).

A stalemate may occur when only two players are left alive who are unable to kill or hang the other or one can prevent the other from killing indefinitely (e.g. a Tavern Keeper versus an Arsonist). In most cases, this will not lead to a draw, but to an automatic victory for one of the remaining factions. This win normally goes for the faction with the lowest win rate.

Faction Victory[]

A faction victory is achieved for either the Town, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing, Vampires, Plaguebearer, or Pestilence if at least one member of one faction is alive while all members of the other factions are dead or converted (by any means). In case of a faction victory, all members of the faction win, regardless of whether or not they are dead or alive.

While Plaguebearer and Pestilence count as different roles, in the case of an Amnesiac remembering one of them and winning the game, the dead player will also win as long as they didn't leave, even if their roles are not the same. Despite having different victory screens, they are essentially the same faction.

 Town Victory[]

This will occur when the Town is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing, and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

If a Vigilante is alive with a Witch, the game will continue until one of them is killed. The game will also not end if there is more than one Witch alive. A Transporter will win in a 1 v 1 situation only against the Mafioso.

 Mafia Victory[]

This will occur when the Mafia is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Coven, Neutral Killing, and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

A Mafia member will win in a 1 v 1 situation against a Tavern Keeper or the Jailor without executions. Additionally, the Godfather wins automatically against a Transporter.

In the Town Traitor game mode, the Town Traitor will win with the Mafia. If the Town Traitor is the last Mafia member left, the Town has 3 Days to hang or kill the Town Traitor, and if this is not done, the Mafia will win. In addition, in a 1 v 1 scenario, the Town Traitor wins automatically against all roles except the Jailor with executions left, a Vigilante, and the Retributionist with a usable Vigilante. The Tavern Keeper wins against a Vigilante, and a Transporter automatically wins against a Vigilante and Retributionist.

 Coven Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This will occur when the Coven is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Neutral Killing, and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

A Coven member will win in a 1 v 1 situation against the Jailor without executions, and any Coven members without Role block Immunity win automatically against a Tavern Keeper. Additionally, the Coven Leader wins automatically against the Godfather, and Medusa wins automatically against a Transporter.

In the Town Traitor game mode, the Town Traitor will win with the Coven. If the Town Traitor is the last Coven member left, the Town has 3 Days to hang or kill the Town Traitor, and if this is not done, the Coven will win. In addition, in a 1 v 1 scenario, the Town Traitor wins automatically against all roles except the Jailor with executions left, a Vigilante, and the Retributionist with a usable Vigilante. The Tavern Keeper wins against a Vigilante, and a Transporter automatically wins against a Vigilante and Retributionist.

In VIP Mode, if the VIP dies at any point, the Coven will win automatically. This will still occur even if all members of the Coven are dead, so long as the VIP died in the same Night.

 Vampire Victory[]

This will occur when the Vampires are the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing, and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate and other Vampires) are dead or converted.

A Vampire will win in a 1 v 1 situation against a Transporter or a Tavern Keeper.

 Serial Killer Victory[]

This will occur when Serial Killers are the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing (excluding other Serial Killers), and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

A Serial Killer will win in a 1 v 1 situation against a Transporter, the Jailor without executions, a Vampire, or the Godfather, Mafioso, or Coven Leader. This is due to its low win rate.

 Arsonist Victory[]

This will occur when Arsonists are the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing (excluding other Arsonists), and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

An Arsonist will win in a 1 v 1 situation against a Tavern Keeper, the Jailor without executions, a Transporter, the Godfather, the Mafioso, a Vampire, or a Serial Killer. An Arsonist will also automatically win against most roles that have no way to stop the Arsonist so that the game does not drag on for an unnecessary amount of time.

Against the Werewolf, both roles are capable of killing each other. If it is a Full Moon Night and ignition happen on the same Night, it is a draw by wipeout. If ignition happens on a non- Full Moon Night, then the Arsonist wins, however this scenario became unlikely as the Werewolf received a buff where the attack restriction is removed 4th Night onwards.

Against the Veteran, mind-games ensue.

 Werewolf Victory[]

This will occur when the Werewolf is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing (excluding the Werewolf), and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

Most deadlocks do not occur with the Werewolf, as they will kill role blockers, kill Transporters and bypass Basic Defense. The Werewolf will win in a 1 v 1 situation against the Godfather, the Mafioso, a Vampire, the Veteran (with or without alerts), or a Serial Killer.

 Juggernaut Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This will occur when the Juggernaut is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing (excluding the Juggernaut), and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate) are dead.

 Plaguebearer Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This will occur when the Plaguebearer is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing, and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate and Plaguebearer) are dead, and they have not yet infected everyone (e.g. an uninfected Survivor and the Plaguebearer are the last players alive).

The win screen used to appear when Pestilence won in general, but an update before the official release of the Coven Expansion gave it a win screen of its own.

 Pestilence Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This will occur when Pestilence is the last standing faction alive, meaning all Townies, Mafia, Coven, Neutral Killing, and Neutral Chaos (excluding the Pirate and Pestilence) are dead.

Non-Faction Victory[]

If all remaining faction members die in the same Night or a draw by timeout occurs, one of the following victory messages will appear. The message does not mean that all players with that role have won. Additionally, these roles may also win during a faction victory, even without their own victory message.

 Witch Victory[]

 | Cannot happen in the Coven Expansion.

This is the result of at least one Witch surviving when all faction members are dead. Only the living Witches will win. Witches do not win during Town victories or during any draw, even if there are no Townies left alive. In any situation with an alive Vigilante or multiple Witches, the game will continue unless either role is killed. In a 1 v 1 situation against a Vigilante without bullets, the Witch will win.

 Executioner Victory[]

This is the result of at least one Executioner winning by having hanged their target earlier in the game. Only the Executioners that have hanged their target will win. Winning by becoming a Jester and being hanged does not apply. The Executioner does not have to be alive to win.

 Jester Victory[]

This is the result of at least one Jester winning by being hanged earlier in the game. Only the Jesters that were hanged will win.

 Survivor Victory[]

This is the result of at least one Survivor surviving during a timeout or wipeout. Only the living Survivors will win.

 Guardian Angel Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This is the result of at least one Guardian Angel winning the game without anyone else winning. Only the Guardian Angels that have kept their target alive will win. This can occur in a draw by timeout while the target of the Guardian Angel is still alive. This may also happen if their target has failed to complete their goal, which is only possible if their starting target is a Pirate or Amnesiac, although Amnesiacs can also become either a Jester or Executioner and lose. The Guardian Angel does not have to be alive to win. Note that if the Guardian Angel died and their target is on the losing side of a stalemate, such as their target being the Godfather when them and a Serial Killer are the only two players left alive, whoever normally wins in the stalemate will win (in this case the Serial Killer), but the Guardian Angel will also win since their target (in this case the Godfather) is still alive, even though the Godfather lost.

 Pirate Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This is the result of the Pirate achieving their goal of successfully plundering two people while the game ends in a timeout or wipeout. The Pirate does not have to be alive to win.

 Neutrals Victory[]

This is the result of no faction winning except for multiple Neutral roles during a wipeout or elimination of all factional roles, such as the last two people alive being a Survivor and a Pirate (victorious or not). Any non-factional Neutrals that are alive will instead have their own win screen shown. If multiple Neutrals are alive, and at least one of them has won, this win screen will appear despite the fact that some alive Neutrals have not won.

Lovers Victory[]

 | Coven Expansion exclusive.

This will occur only when the remaining players alive are lovers of each other in Lovers Mode. Lover pairs can be of any faction as long as only pairs are alive. If any non-lover players are alive, the game continues. Deceased pairs of lovers will not win the game, but the Pirate can still win the game if they completed their goal before only lover pairs remain.

Draw[]

A draw occurs when no player has successfully completed their goal. In a timeout, if any non-factional role, such as a Survivor, Jester, Executioner, Pirate, or Guardian Angel exists, has survived or completed their goal, their win screen shows instead and they will be the only winners of the game - everyone else will lose.

Any Amnesiacs or Witches that survive in the event of a draw will not win, but rather lose, as the former is expected to remember a role (formerly, they could win without remembering and had their own win screen, but this was removed before the graphic redesign) and the latter is expected to see the Town lose the game (since the Town does not lose in the event of a draw even if all Townies are dead).

Note that the Neutral Killing roles and the Plaguebearer lose during draws no matter what, this may be a bug.

Wipeout[]

A wipeout occurs when all players are dead. This means no members of the Town, Mafia, Coven, or any Neutrals are alive. In this case, factional roles will get a draw, while non-factional roles will lose (as they are unable to draw). Those that complete their goal but are dead will have their win screen shown instead, and everyone else will lose.

Timeout[]

The game ends because there have been no deaths or conversions for two consecutive Days and Nights, and must be late in the game (at least Day 9). There will be a notification on the preceding Day when this is about to happen. If the Vampires bite the last non- Vampire Town member on a Night where the game would otherwise end in a draw, the game will end in a Vampire victory, not a draw even though there is no death halting the timer in clear recognition of the victory. However, a conversion will not prevent a draw if there are other factional non- Vampires remaining.

Stalemate[]

In the event that the final two players happen to be of opposing factions, the stalemate detector may activate, rewarding an automatic win to one of those players or factions. If a Jester was hanged during the Day, the game will not end in a stalemate, because a Jester's haunt can break any deadlock in this list. A player being Trapped also prevents the stalemate from being activated. Victory Conditions[]

On occasion, factions may cooperate with each other if their victory conditions specify so. If a member of a different faction is left alive by the end of the game and the winning faction's victory conditions allow for that faction to win with them, then that player would achieve a victory along with the winning faction. For example, if a Witch were to still be alive at the end of a game where the Mafia win, then the Witch would also achieve a victory along with them. The victory screen at the end will always prioritize a faction's win instead of non-factional wins, so if the Mafia and a Survivor win together, the Mafia victory screen will be displayed.

There are three types of victory conditions:

Wins with: You will achieve a victory with the role in question at the end of the game.

Must kill: You will not be able to achieve a victory until every member of the certain role is dead (unless there is a stalemate, in which case see above).

May spare: By the end of the game, if this role is still alive when you achieve a victory, they may not achieve it with you. However, you do not need to kill this role in order to achieve a victory.

Note: Roles that are listed as "may spare" can still win with your role at the end of the game if they have specific victory conditions that allow them to do so, e.g. a Jester.

Exceptions:

The Town and Witches must kill each other only if a Vigilante with bullets is alive or if more than one Witch is alive.

However, instead of hunting for the Witch (in the case there is only one), it may be easier to hang or kill any Vigilantes remaining, so the Town automatically wins without having to find the Witch. This can be risky, only do this if you are sure there are no other opposing factions alive or multiple Witches.

Multiple Neutral Killings can win with each other if they are the same role like a faction (e.g. one Serial Killer can win with another Serial Killer), but must kill other Neutral Killings with different roles if presented.

The Plaguebearer and Pestilence can win with each other, provided an Amnesiac becomes either of them.

 Witch does not exist in the Coven Expansion and therefore cannot win with any of the Coven Exclusive roles.
