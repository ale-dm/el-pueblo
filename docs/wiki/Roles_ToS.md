# Roles (ToS)

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Roles_(ToS)

There are currently 35 roles in Classic Town of Salem, separated into 3 categories: Town, Mafia, and Neutral. These include 8 unique roles.

Users who own Town of Salem - The Coven have 50 roles to play with, 18 which are unique. This expansion includes the Coven faction, extra Town roles, and special Neutral roles.

 Town Roles

The Town roles each have one goal: "Hang every criminal and evildoer." Those evildoers include the Mafia, Neutral Killing, Neutral Chaos (except Pirate), the Coven, and Witches.

Notes

Becoming a certain role is entirely up to chance, depending on the list of your game mode. Scrolls allow you to increase the chance of becoming a certain role.

The ideas for the Forger, Vampire, Werewolf, Hypnotist and Necromancer roles were originally suggested by the community.
