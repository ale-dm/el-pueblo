# Abilities (ToS)

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Abilities_(ToS)

In Town of Salem, most roles in the game have Night abilities, e.g. investigating, killing, or protecting. Abilities are a key part to the game. Most abilities are Night abilities and can only be used during the Night. Some roles have few or no abilities, but have special Attributes instead, such as the Executioner. The Jailor, Mayor, Pirate, and a dead Medium have Day abilities.

 Night Abilities[]

To use your Night ability, click the button next to a person's name during the Night. (You can sort the table below by clicking on the small arrow next to a heading. Some roles, like a Transporter or Disguiser, require two buttons to be clicked for their Night ability.) Your action will be done based on your role's priority. Image example is the Bodyguard's ability.

Priority is the order in which a role's action happens during the Night. The lower the number, the earlier in the Night the action occurs. Abilities with higher priority may affect abilities with a lower priority (for specifics, see Roles}}). Day abilities always have a higher priority than Night abilities. Some roles will still be unaffected by higher-priority abilities because of the role's attributes or qualities.

If an ability causes a visit, a Lookout can see the visitor, an Alert Veteran/ Stone Gazing Medusa will attack visitors that visited them, an Ambusher or Crusader will attack the visitor, a Trap on the target will trigger, and the Werewolf/ Pestilence will attack visitors to their target.

Ability Types[]

Every roles abilities can be classified into 6 different types. Investigative, Protective, Killing, Roleblocking, Manipulation and Disruption. Retributionists and Necromancers will take on the ability type of the player they raise.

Investigation[]

Investigation is an ability is used by roles to gain information about other players' roles or actions. This is an ability that usually can be disrupted by other roles, such as a Transporter or Disguiser. You may see the table below for the list of all roles that possess this ability type directly or indirectly.

It should be noted that the Trapper is not a Town Investigative role, its main point is to protect people. The information of what roles visited your protected person is only side info, therefore making it an Investigative/Protective hybrid role.

 Janitors can be considered to have an Investigative ability to their cleaning. If a Tavern Keeper gets killed by a Serial Killer for roleblocking them, you may read what is a normally bloodied will.

Roleblocking[]

Roleblocking is a form of disruption where a player's Night ability is 'blocked' by another player that can roleblock. This causes the target to not be able to use their ability, and thus their ability having no effect for the current Night. This can be overridden by roles with role-block immunity. Super roleblocking is very similar to roleblocking, but with a few differences, being that a Serial Killer can't kill their original target, and they can kill the targets they roleblock.
