# Icon

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Icon

Lobby Icons are cosmetics next to players' names in lobbies. Other people and you can see them in the lobby.

When you join a game lobby, in the player list, your name will have an icon next to it. You can choose which Icon you want displayed in your Customization Options.

Trivia

The Legacy Season icons may be based on tier rewards for the game League of Legends.

The Ranked Season 5 Icons are based off of the Horseman of the Apocalypse (assumably Pestilence).

As of now, it takes exactly 5,200 Town Points to purchase every single Lobby Icon without Merit Points. It sums up to approximately $10 USD based on the currency's root value (260 Town Points equals $1 USD).
