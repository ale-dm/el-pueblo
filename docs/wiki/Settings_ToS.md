# Settings (ToS)

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Settings_(ToS)

Settings icon

You can change the game's default settings by clicking the cogwheel button on the main menu. While in a game, you can only change a few settings with the User Interface. You can also change these settings in a lobby. 

 | Tab

 | Option

 | Description

 | Sound

 | Music

 | Activate or deactivate music. You can change the volume of the music as well. You can turn it on or off during the game with the user interface.

 | Effects

 | Activate or deactivate sound effects. You can change the volume of the sound effects as well. You can turn them on or off during the game with the user interface.

 | Chat

 | Preferred Chat Language

 | Change your preferred chat language which will affect which queues you will be placed in. Only English, Turkish (Türkçe), and Spanish (Español) are currently available.

 | Show Player Numbers

 | Enable to show position number for players chatting in the game chat.

 | Show Extended Player Numbers

 | Enable to show position number for players that are referenced in messages.

 | Enable Chat Filter

 | Activate or deactivate Chat Filter. This will replace many words commonly considered swear words by inoffensive variants (e.g. balderdash, tarnation, fopdoodle).

 | Display

 | Toggle Fullscreen

 | Activate or deactivate full-screen mode. You can turn it on or off during the game with the user interface.

 | Enable VSync

 | Enable to sync game frame rate to monitor refresh rate.

 | General

 | Hide My Customizations From Other Players

 | When activated, only you will see the cosmetic customizations you have currently selected. All other players will only see the default avatars.

 | Show Classic Skins Only

 | When activated, you will not see other players' cosmetic customizations. They will only have the default avatars.

 | Hide All Pets

 | When activated, you will not see any pets during the game.

 | Show Environmental Effects

 | Enable to see snow, embers, etc. on supported Maps.

 | Show Toaster Notifications

 | Enable to show incoming chat messages and game invites in a toaster notification.

 | Autoexpand Game Windows When Space Available

 | Enable to expand chat window and player window when other windows are minimized and there is available space.

 | Clear Prefs

 | Similar to clearing cookies in a browser. Clears saved username, password, and more.

 | Help

 | Facebook

 | Visit BlankMediaGames' Facebook page.

 | YouTube

 | Visit BlankMediaGames' YouTube page.

 | Twitter

 | Visit BlankMediaGames' Twitter page.

 | Steam

 | Visit BlankMediaGames' Steam Community page.

 | View Roles

 | Opens the Role Library to show all of the roles in Town of Salem. If Coven Expansion is owned, will display Coven Expansion roles as well.

 | Get Help

 | Visit BlankMediaGames' help page.

 | Confirm

 | Confirm your email address.

 | Report Bug

 | Visit the forums to report a bug.

 | Enable Player Log

 | Enable to enable the writing of a player log.

 | Delete

 | Delete your Town of Salem account.
