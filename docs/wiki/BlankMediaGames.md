# BlankMediaGames

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/BlankMediaGames

Aside from the online games, BlankMediaGames has also made two card games based on their first game: "Town of Salem - The Card Game" and "Town of Salem's The Savior of Salem".

History[]

BlankMediaGames was founded in February 2014 by Josh Brittain and Blake Burns. A Kickstarter campaign began on February 14, 2014 to develop Town of Salem. The company, based in Round Rock, Texas, was composed of a small team of game enthusiasts consisting of five programmers, one community manager, one quality assurance manager, one product manager, and two artists (one who focused on UI and avatars, the other on backgrounds and houses). Despite being spread across the globe, they were united by their passion for gaming.

In July 2024, Digital Bandidos acquired BlankMediaGames as well as the Town of Salem franchise; BlankMediaGames appears to have been dissolved since Digital Bandidos has taken over development of the games.

Data Breach[]

From November to December of 2018, a team of hackers gained access to BlankMediaGames' system by gaining access to the Reddit of one of the cofounders. Then, he gained access to Town of Salem's internal system, and gained access to 8,388,894 email addresses, and over 2,000,000 passwords. These were then used to gain access to other information, such as names, email addresses, and financial details. After that, BlankMediaGames tightened security, encouraging everyone to change their passwords.

Trivia[]

BlankMediaGames attempted pushing out Town of Salem in 2013 by using Kickstarter. The goal was to achieve $20,000. Unfortunately, the Kickstarter project was unsuccessful with 63 backers and $1,375 pledged. It was advertised on Starcraft II Mafia's website.

BlankMediaGames has released Official Town of Salem Stories for Town of Salem. The most recent story, "The Mysterious Being," can be viewed by clicking on an icon in the top-right corner. Previous stories were archived on BlankMediaGames's website, but the page was taken down.
