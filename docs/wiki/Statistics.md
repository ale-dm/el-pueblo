# Statistics

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Statistics

The Statistics icon

 Statistics were added in Version 1.5.0 to track players' wins and specific actions with some roles. If a certain statistic is clicked, it also shows a leaderboard with the top 10 players in that statistic. It is accessed via by a button in the left side of the screen, below the Achievement button.

Statistics were removed to fix some bugs and issues with the switch to the Unity version of the game. They were added back in Version 3.3.0.

Notes[]

Do note that the Statistics section is outdated. As such, statistics such as hearing a private whisper are shown under Spy, despite the Blackmailer being the only role to hear whispers, along with all roles exclusive to the Coven Expansion (+ Ambusher and Hypnotist) not having other statistics. A full list of roles with outdated statistics are as follows:

 Spy - No longer can hear private whispers, needs bugging-related statistics.

 Blackmailer - Needs private whisper statistics.

 Tavern Keeper/ Bootlegger - Needs "Number of Times You Visited A Werewolf".

 Sheriff - Now only sees targets as "suspicious" or "seems innocent".

 Witch - Needs "Number of Times Your Mystical Barrier Saved You From Death".

 Retributionist - No longer brings someone back to life.
