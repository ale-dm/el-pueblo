 Mafia Returns[]

It took The Don far too long to realize that the Witch had abandoned him. Ever since she and that fiend Helena went into that portal and came back with those other witches, members of his Mafia had been killed of under mysterious circumstances.

First, it was the Janitor, who had succumbed to a mysterious poison. Then, it was the Consigliere, who had been found one morning turned into stone.

Finally, one Night, the Witch violently took control of his will, forcing him to kill his own Mafioso. He survived, of course. He always did. But he knew that the Witch no longer needed the Mafia. "That ain't our Witch anymore," he said. "That's the Coven's Leader."

So The Don decided he knew what he had to do. One Night, when the Coven Leader had been gone, presumably hauled off to that jail cell he had grown so used to, he entered her home, searching through her mystical possessions.

"Not 'ere, not 'ere, not 'ere... Aha!"

With a shout of triumph he pulled it out: the powder that had been used to bring him 260 years into the past. Taking a small pinch of the ashes, he threw them high into the air. He knew where he needed to go; back home.

 The Don stepped through the portal and arrived back in Sicily. And this was the Sicily of his time.

His home looked exactly as he had left it. The guards standing outside showed no surprise on seeing him again. He went up to one of them and asked, "How long 've I been gone?"

"Why, you left just 'n hour ago!" they said.

With a laugh, he entered the building. He had spent far too long away from this place. And yet, by that demonic magic, he'd arrived just after he left!

Now, he needed to make sure that the people of his Town were still doing what they needed to.

A week went by, and for once he was actually feared, as he should be. But every Night, he dreamed of that place. The place he never wanted to go back to.

But he knew that he couldn't just leave those witches, after all they did to him. He'd get his revenge on the Town, after all they'd done to him. But he wouldn't be able to defeat them alone.

So he went over to those guards who were most loyal to him, who he'd been forced to leave behind. He told them he'd be needing them on a special mission, and taking another pinch of the powder, he threw it into the air, focusing his mind on the Coven Leader.

The four of them walked through the portal, The Don, his Ambusher, his Hypnotist, and that other one whose role he couldn't quite remember. None of them looked back. But if they did, they might have known they were being followed.

Earlier that Day, the Psychic woke up with a burning feeling in her head. She'd had an omen last Night, and her intuition was always right. 

She went to her neighbor's house. He was a skilled Tracker; he and his hound were always able to sniff out where their target was headed.

Telling him the omen she had, the two of them set out on the journey, his dog Bleu not far behind. And when they arrived, they saw The Don leading his men through a portal. She could feel the psychic resonance coming through that portal, and so they dashed through, ready to solve whatever mystery awaited them.

As soon as the Psychic passed through into the past, she could immediately figure out that there was great evil in this place. As she went to sleep, her crystal ball reflected in the light of the stars, giving her increased clarity as to who the evildoers were.

The next Day, she declared to the Townspeople: "One of these three people must be evildoers!" The Town was naturally skeptical, but they needed all the information they could get.

One of the people had already been found suspicious by someone claiming to be the Sheriff, so he was quickly convicted and executed. However, they were not an evildoer, but rather an innocent Trapper, who hadn't even had time to lay a single trap. The Executioner laughed maniacally, as the Town came to realize they'd hung one of their own.

That Night, the Tracker picked up the scent of one of the remaining two people, hoping that they'd be the evildoer from the Psychic's vision. But as he entered the house, a figure leaped from the shadows, slitting his throat with his blade.

The next morning, the Executioner was also found dead, with a smile on his face. The marks of a scimitar, from the high seas! When the Townspeople checked his house, they found all of his valuables taken. The Townsfolk were pleased at this, the Pirate who cared not what his victims were, as long as he got his loot.

That Night, the Jailor jailed the third person in the Psychic's vision, convinced that he was The Don. The Don told the Jailor that he was the Pirate, and that he would take the loot from any Mafia members he saw. 

The Hypnotist knew that since nobody would be attacked, The Don would hang the next Day. So, she went over to the Lookout's home, and convinced him that he had been attacked, but that she had saved him. 

The Town praised her, but they didn't know that there was, in fact, no Doctor to be found. But for many Nights she went around Town, deceiving Town members into believing her lies.

And yet, eventually, this came to a stop. One Night, the Town filled with acrid green smoke, everyone infected with a mysterious disease.

The Town and Mafia were in a panic, their rivalry now seemed to vanish in the face of this overwhelming threat. The Jailor declared his identity and announced that John Hathorne would soon meet his blade.

That Night, the Mafia cowered at home, too afraid of the disease to do anything.

But when dawn rose over the sky, John Hathorne was alive, not a scratch to be seen. The Jailor, on the other hand, was dead, his body having succumbed to disease. The Town knew that no mortal could survive the Jailor's blade. So, he had to be Pestilence, Horseman of the Apocalypse.

He may have survived the Mafia's bullets, and the Jailor's axe. But not even he could survive the gallows. The smoke cleared away, and everyone let out a sigh of relief. However, that Night, another Townie fell to the Mafia, the Lookout having been ambushed before he could see what had happened. They asked the Doctor why she hadn't saved them, and all she did was laugh.

And as The Don claimed the life of the Psychic, the Pirate had sailed away, taking with him the gold star that the former Sheriff had worn.

The Hypnotist and LixieZixie[]

The Hypnotist was concerned. The Godfather had warned her about this immense danger, and yet the danger was gone. She asked the Godfather if they could return back to their time. "Not yet," he said. "These ain't the folks we were worried about." 

So the Hypnotist set out, along with the Mafioso, to find those who had caused such trouble to the Godfather. "Their minds stand no chance against me." 

But that Night, while she slept, she was attacked. But, despite this, someone had nursed her back to health. And it was no trick. Nobody else knew her craft. 

The next morning, someone whispered to her, "That Serial Killer went after you last Night, but I made sure he didn't kill you!"

That Day, the Mayor revealed himself to the Town, and began interrogating the Townsfolk. But the Day ended before anyone could be Hanged.

That Night, the Mafioso suggested that they kill the Doctor. However, the Hypnotist said not to. After all, she'd make her know that she was another one, by "healing" her. So the Mafioso decided to instead kill someone else, who turned out to be the Crusader.

 Days went by, and eventually the Serial Killer faced his end on the gallows. But that Night, a fire swept through the Town, incinerating many of the houses. Even the Mayor was not safe from the blaze. 

In the end, the only three people remaining were the Hypnotist, the Doctor, and someone else. They both knew that this was the Arsonist, and they found an empty can of gasoline with him after he hanged.

But the sun set, and the Hypnotist had the Mafioso's gun in her hand. But she cast it to the floor, saying "The Doctor did nothing but heal me. She does not deserve this fate."

On the other side of Town, the Potion Master held a harmful potion in her hand. But she did not use it. Instead she ignored the Necronomicon's cries, using the revealing potion on her fellow Doctor. "She did nothing but heal me. She does not deserve this fate."

But, as she realized, her target was skilled at disrupting others. She was a Hypnotist! She felt as if her actions triggered a trap, but she knew that was just the Hypnotist's work.

The next morning, they were both alive. The Potion Master approached the Hypnotist and told her the truth. "I'm not really a Doctor," she admitted, "I'm the Potion Master." 

The Hypnotist told her that she was also not a Doctor, but the Potion Master already seemed to know.

Was there ever really a Doctor in this Town?

The Necronomicon[]

The year was 1630. The Town of Salem had been founded just four years prior, and things were moving smoothly. Town Roles had been established the year before, and things were in full swing. Just outside the Sheriff's office, a Lookout was on watch. A few Days prior, a note had been sent to the Sheriff threatening to kill him for his "malpractice" after he had sentenced a local to the gallows for conspiracy against the Town. Such a thing hadn't been heard of until this point, but the Sheriff alleged that the woman wanted to overthrow the newly introduced hierarchy of the Town by poisoning the Mayor with the highly poisonous berries that resided nearby. Thing was, the Sheriff only had circumstantial evidence, yet the Town believed his case enough to drown out the non-believers, and the noose cracked.

The Lookout was there that Day, when a few Town members attempted to Hang the Sheriff after what he had done. Under threat of vote by the Mayor, their anger simmered down, but it seems one of them considered themselves a Vigilante. If they were to come tonight as the Lookout suspected, they wouldn't be getting away without facing the Town's judgement. The cover of the Night wouldn't fool the Lookout's eyes. Just after 3am, the Lookout eyed someone, approaching the Sheriff's house. They went in, and came out just a minute later, but they stopped just outside the door. Their head turned to look at the bush the Lookout was disguised in. Out of fear, the Lookout hunched back slightly, enough to cause a rustle. The Vigilante turned their gun, when the Lookout stood up and yelled to stop.

V: Why should I?

L: You're making a mistake. You just shot the Sheriff, didn't you?

V: Course I did, he had it coming. He had no evidence to hang that woman, and you know it.

L: But he's the confirmed Sheriff, are you insane? You can't shoot authority like that!

V: A confirmed Sheriff you say? Do you all just believe that anyone who's "a member of the Town" is just instantly completely innocent? Void of all wrongdoing and corruption?

L: Well, no, but-

V: Yet you still defend them like a flock of sheep. You all believe things are black and white, don't you? Green guys and red guys, is that it?

L: Murder is morally wrong.

V: And that's exactly what he did, even if it wasn't by his hand. Just shows how much of a coward he was.

L: I won't let you get away with this.

V: Of course you won't. You'll chase me to the ends of the earth. I'm not saying that man wasn't the Sheriff, I'm saying he was a murderer. Are you seriously too dense to not realise someone can be one and the other at the same time? Look, I've seen true evil. Now I can tell when that same traitorous spirit resides in any man, and take care of it before it becomes too much of an issue. Want a demonstration?

The Vigilante pulled out a book from under his cloak. He handed it to the Lookout, who reeled at the putrid state of the book.

L: What... is this? A book? What the hell happened to it?

V: Read it.

L: What's in it?

V: The darkness you claim doesn't exist in your very souls, given form. Go on, read it.

L: Will you let me go if I do?

V: ...Sure.

After a few seconds, staring down at the book, the Lookout finally opened it. Gazing inside, he couldn't comprehend what he was looking at. His peripheral vision starting bending backward. He felt like he was closer and closer to the pages of the book, until his face was pressed against them. Suddenly, it had seemed he had went through the book itself, yet everything was dark. That thought was the last he had, as the book closed behind his back, and he was lost to the void. The book fell to the floor, before being picked up by the Vigilante.

V: ...One of these Days, I'll be dead. I won't be able to do your work anymore. I'll give you enough power to lie in wait, until the perfect time to strike this Town and cast ultimate judgement. It's the right thing to do. This damn Town is cursed with ignorance. You'll bring the truth, I know.

The Vigilante put the Necronomicon back into his cloak, before walking off into the dark fog. He shot as many people as he could before the Medium ratted him out, but it was enough. The Necronomicon, buried under a hill the Day prior to his Hanging, slept to conserve its strength, before the right time to strike. That time came 34 years later to the Day, when the Coven first struck Salem.

 Townies[]

The Townies have been around for as long as the Town of Salem has been. Whoever built the Town has long since passed away, but the Town Roles they used to assign to their Townies have been passed down generation to generation, with a few new iterations showing up and new roles appearing from the brilliant minds that occasionally graced the Town. Legend has it, the very first " Town" that Salem had was completely free of criminals and evildoers. Unfortunately, all good things must come to an end.

The Town had already been through one wave of evil years back from now. Somehow, the Town survived the atrocious evils that passed through, even overcoming the blood red Nights that the Necronomicon brought upon Salem a few Days after it was introduced. They thought they had banished both the Necronomicon and the criminals from their Town for good, yet fate had other ideas.

The Necronomicon has returned, backed by a horrific onslaught of the Town's past adversaries, their goals conjoined under red moonlight, and signed in scarlet fire by their founder, the Necromancer in the absence of their Coven Leader. Once more, they've blended their monochrome hearts into the gold of the Town's purest souls, and few can tell the difference. Murder is on the horizon, and the Necronomicon has an iron grip of the whole Town, capable of suffocating the Town with horrible Spells that can spell doom for them all. Without the distrust and conflicting objectives that kept the evildoers apart before, the Traitors were stronger than ever.

If the Town has any hope to return to its former peace, the Witch Trials must return, and the Necronomicon must be banished again, for good. Unfortunately, the rude awakening of the Necronomicon has left the Town in no state to perform the ritual. Now, they must complete the preparations and banish the Necronomicon from Salem forever, before the efforts of them and their ancestors are reduced to dust by the Traitors in Salem.

 Traitors[]

The Coven was haphazardly formed during the Town of Salem's quarrels with the Mafia and the neutral individuals who were forwarding their own agenda. The leader of the Coven, the soon-to-be Archmage, was formerly a Witch that used to assist any other factions that were against the Town. The Witch despised the Town for many reasons, and wanted to live to see them burn, which is why they decided to break off from the tactic of jeopardizing their identity by attempting to contact other factions, and instead create their very own as the Coven Leader.

Every member of the Coven was fished from a different sector of the game that was life. They all started innocent, but were led down the path of malice for one reason or another. Some members didn't even hate the Town, instead driven by selfish desires, and while the Coven Leader didn't consider themselves selfish, her Coven was already low on members, so they were tolerated. When the Town finally banished the Necronomicon from Salem, the Coven Leader just left. Not a word of appreciation or farewell was found. She disappeared in the same manner as her victims.

While a few members of the Coven left for other ventures, three members stayed, most importantly, the Necromancer. The Necromancer, along with their two associates, began seeking and recruiting whatever evil still lingered in the land around Salem. The Arsonist willingly joined the Traitors, the Serial Killer found his way in and among other guests, the Necromancer managed to make a few recruits out of lingering souls and unsettled qualms. Anything malleable in them was abused. In the end, they had 15 members, and with the return of the Necronomicon, it was their time to act. This time, the Town would finally burn to the ground. With the Traitors working as one and the Necronomicon watching, there was surely no hope.

Despite the Coven Leader's absence, the present Coven members still somehow felt her presence. They could still feel her delight, poisoning the air... why?
