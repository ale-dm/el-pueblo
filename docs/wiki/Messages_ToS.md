Things to note[]

A Necromancer can receive most of the roles' messages by using them when in the graveyard.

A Retributionist can receive most Town roles' messages by using them when in the graveyard.

A Witch/ Coven Leader may receive some of these messages through the feedback they receive from their target.

If you see a small circled Hypnotist icon next to a message's description , then a Hypnotist can produce that message as well through their ability.

If you see a small circled Any/ Cleaned/ Stoned icon next to a message's description , then another role (other than the ones listed above) can produce that message as well.

Anything in a message surrounded by () is a varying piece of text.

"#" surrounded by () indicates that the message uses a number that may change in other scenarios.

Two or more words/strings separated by " / " surrounded by () indicates that the message uses one of those words/strings and that it may change in other scenarios.

"Message" surrounded by () indicates that the message uses the contents of what a player types.

"Username" surrounded by () indicates that the message uses a player's account name.

"Player" or the name of a role surrounded by () indicates that the message uses a player's name.

Messages containing names may have that player's position (number) before their name if you enable "Show Extended Player Numbers" in the Settings, unless specified otherwise.

Depending on the avatar's gender, cause of death messages will change (e.g. "He was killed by a member of the Mafia." compared to "She was killed by a member of the Mafia.").

To not clutter the page, all of the death causes will be written with "(S)He".

If you die from 2 causes, your cause of death message will change to the "also" version (e.g. "She apparently committed suicide. She was also killed by a member of the Mafia.").

If you die from 3 or more causes, only one death message will display and the others will show up as "additional ways" (e.g. "He was stabbed by a Serial Killer. He also died 2 additional ways.") on the top-middle of the screen.

However, each cause of death is listed separately in the chat log.

 | (Player) died last night.

Displays for everyone during the Night-Discussion phase transition when someone dies. This message appears in both the chat log and top-middle of the screen. Causes of death can be found on this page for the respective role.

 | We could not find a last will.

Displays for everyone when a dead player either does not have a Last Will, was Cleaned, Stoned, or forged to not have a Last Will. This happens even if hanged. Also appears in the top-middle of the screen.

 | We found a will next to their body.

Displays for everyone when a dead player's Last Will is about to be revealed (if they had one) or they were forged to have a Last Will. This happens even if hanged. Also appears on the top-middle of the screen.

 | We found a death note next to their body.

Displays for everyone when a dead player's killer's Death Note is about to be revealed (if they had one). Also appears on the top-middle of the screen.

 | Their last will was covered in blood and unreadable.

Displays for everyone during the Night-Discussion phase when a Tavern Keeper or Bootlegger role blocks a non- Cautious Serial Killer, a Jailor who did not execute a non- Cautious Serial Killer, or a Pirate who loses a duel to a non- Cautious Serial Killer. Also appears on the top-middle of the screen.

 | (Player)'s role was (Role).

Displays for everyone when a dead player's role is about to be revealed or they were forged to reveal a different role. This happens even if hanged. Also appears on the top-middle of the screen.

Displays for any role who saves their Last Will.

 | Your death note has been saved.

Displays for roles with a Death Note when they save it.

Displays to all players when a player votes for a player on trial.

 | (Player) has changed their vote.

Displays to all players when a player changes their vote for a player on trial.

Displays after the Judgement phase when a player voted guilty on another player.

 | (Player) voted innocent.

Displays after the Judgement phase when a player voted innocent on another player.

 | (Player) abstained.

Displays after the Judgement phase when a player did not vote on another player.

 | The Town has decided to hang (Player) by a vote of (Guilty votes) to (Innocent votes).

Displays to everyone at the beginning of the Last Words phase, when a player begins the process of hanging. Also appears in the top-middle of the screen.

 | The Town has decided to pardon (Player) by a vote of (Innocent votes) to (Guilty votes).

Displays to everyone when a player is pardoned from trial. Also appears in the top-middle of the screen.

 | You did not perform your night ability.

Displays at the end of the Night for a player with a Night Ability who did not select a target during the Night.

 | You did not perform your day ability.

Displays at the start of the Night for a player with a Day ability who did not select a target during the Day.

 | An unstoppable force meets an invincible object. The ground beneath your feet shudders!

Displays for everyone at the end of the Night if Pestilence and a fully powered up Juggernaut attack each other.

 | You cannot whisper yourself, that would be weird.

Displays for yourself when you attempt to whisper yourself.

 | You cannot ignore yourself. That would be weird.

Displays for yourself when you attempt to ignore yourself.

 | You cannot unignore yourself. That would be weird.

Displays for yourself when you attempt to unignore yourself.

 | Someone attacked you but your defense was too strong!

Displays to a role with Defense when someone with a lower Attack value attacks them. This message will also appear for a Vampire Hunter who was visited by a Vampire.

 | Your target's defense was too strong to kill.

Displays to an attacking role when their target has a higher Defense than the attacker's Attack value. This message will also appear if the target was healed, and attacking a Witch when they still have their mystical barrier will not generate this message.

 | You cannot whisper in a VIP game.

Displays when a player attempts to whisper in VIP Mode.

 | Your lover died. You can feel your heart breaking.

Displays to a player when their lover dies in Lovers Mode. (except Pestilence, Pestilence receives a special message when their lover dies)

 | A report has been filed against (Player).

Displays for yourself when you successfully report someone.

 | Moderator has banned (Player).

Displays for everyone when a player who is currently in the game gets banned or suspended, either manually or got voted guilty in a Trial System report.

 | (Player) has left the game.

Displays for everyone when a player leaves the game.

 | (S)He apparently committed suicide.

Cause of death for a player who has left the game.

 | (S)He died of a broken heart. or (S)He also died of a broken heart.

Cause of death for a player when their lover dies in Lovers Mode. (except Pestilence, Pestilence receives a special message when their lover dies)

 | (Player), (Player) (has / have) won.

Displays at the end of the game. Only the winning faction's members and any victorious non-factional players will be displayed, each player being separated by a comma and space.

 | If no one dies by tomorrow the game will end in a draw.

Displays at the beginning of the Discussion phase when no deaths have occured ( Days 7+).

 | The game has ended in a draw.

Displays at the end of the game when all opposing factions are eliminated/no deaths have occured in 2 consecutive Days ( Days 7+). Any victorious non-factional players will NOT be displayed.
