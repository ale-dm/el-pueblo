# Graveyard

Fuente: https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Graveyard

The Graveyard is a recollection of dead players throughout the game. The player's name, along with their role, will appear there in the order they died. When a player dies, the cause(s) of their death will be announced and placed in the Chat, and then in the Graveyard. However, there are attributes to this Graveyard that makes it crucial for the game.
The Graveyard is important in game modes such as Ranked and Ranked Practice because it helps narrow down claim space and makes it easier to find and hang evildoers, and see what roles are still alive. You can also click on a dead player's role to see their Last Will and Death Note if one is left behind. Every time a player dies, their names are added to the Graveyard immediately after death, including hanging or leaving the game.

A Graveyard with no players

On the Web and Steam versions, it is located on the upper-left corner of the screen and can be minimized. On the mobile version, tapping on the tab with the tombstone icon will show up the Graveyard.

Roles that interact with the Graveyard[]

 Amnesiacs[]

 Amnesiacs must choose a role from the Graveyard to remember a role and be able to win.

Because of Amnesiacs, it is possible to have two or more unique roles in the Graveyard.

If the Amnesiac remembers that they were an Amnesiac, they will get the "I am who I am" achievement.

If the Amnesiac remembers that they were a Survivor, they will get the "Still Neutral" achievement.

If the Amnesiac remembers that they were a member of the Mafia, they will get the "Taking Sides" achievement.

 Forgers[]

If a Forger forges a player who has died the same Night, their role and Last Will will be replaced as whatever the forgery is.

If an Amnesiac tries to remember a forged role, they will become the forged player's true role.

If the forged role is reanimated by the Necromancer or resurrected by the Retributionist, they will use the abilities of their true role on the secondary target.

 Forgers only have 2 forgeries.

Because of Forgers, it is possible to have two or more unique roles in the Graveyard.

If the Forger forges someone, they get the "Counterfeit Connoisseur" achievement.

If the Forger forges an Investigator, they get the "Derailed" achievement.

If the Forger forges themselves, they get the "Could Be Worse?" achievement.

If the Forger forges a Mafia member, they get the "Whoops!" achievement.

 Janitors[]

If a Janitor cleans a player who has died the same Night, their role will be shown as Cleaned in the Graveyard, and their Last Will is deleted.

The Janitor however will secretly know what the role and Last Will of the Cleaned target was.

 Janitors only have 3 cleanings.

 Cleaned players cannot be remembered by Amnesiacs, reanimated by the Necromancer or the Retributionist. Prior to the Retributionist rework, Cleaned Townies couldn't be revived by the Retributionist.

If the Janitor wins the game without cleaning any bodies, they will get the "On Strike" achievement.

If the Janitor cleans themselves, they will get the "Impossible!" achievement.

 Mediums[]

 Mediums can communicate with dead players every Night.

If the Medium dies, they can use a seance to communicate with a living player the next day.

If the Medium seances an Arsonist, they get the "Talk to a Pyro" achievement.

If the Medium seances the Godfather, they get the "Talk to the Mob" achievement.

If the Medium seances a Serial Killer, they get the "Talk to a Psychopath" achievement.

 Medusas[]

If Medusa uses a Stone Gaze and they are visited, the player(s) that visited will be dealt a Powerful Attack, and if they successfully die, their role will be shown as Stoned in the Graveyard, and their Last Will is deleted. If Medusa has the Necronomicon, she may apply the same effects to anyone she visits.

If a player is both Stoned and Cleaned on the same Night, then they will show up as Cleaned in the Graveyard.

If a player is both Stoned and forged on the same Night, then they will show up as Stoned in the Graveyard.

 Medusa only has 3 Stone Gazes for visitors.

 Stoned players cannot be remembered by Amnesiacs, reanimated by the Necromancer or the Retributionist. Prior to the Retributionist rework, Stoned Townies couldn't be revived by the Retributionist.

If Medusa turns 3 people into stone in a single Night, they will get the "A Rockin Party" achievement.

If Medusa turns a revealed Mayor into stone, they will get the "Stone Wall" achievement.

 Necromancers[]

The Necromancer uses dead player's roles to affect other players in the game.

The Necromancer can only use a role once, and dead players will not be told their role has been used.

Unique roles such as Pestilence and Werewolf will be able to tell because of their death message coming up.

Reanimating dead roles can yield information if they are a Town Investigative role, protect someone else for a Night if they are a Town Protective role, or attack someone by using roles such as a Mafioso or Serial Killer.

If the Necromancer reanimates a Werewolf on a Full Moon, they get the "Blood Moon" achievement.

If the Necromancer reanimates a Pestilence, they get the "Extinct Disease" achievement.

They will also die from doing this unless a Doctor, Potion Master or Guardian Angel heals them.

If the Necromancer reanimates a Doctor and heals any Coven member, they get the "Revived and Rejuvenated" achievement.

 Retributionists[]

The Retributionist can 'reanimate' (resurrect) just like the Necromancer, but only on Townies.

The Retributionist can only use a Townie once, and dead players will not be told their role has been used.

Resurrecting dead Townies can yield information if they are a Town Investigative role, protect someone else for a Night if they are a Town Protective role, or attack someone by using roles such as a Vigilante or Vampire Hunter.

If the Retributionist resurrects a Doctor to heal themselves, they get the "Welcome Back Doc" achievement.

If the Retributionist resurrects a Sheriff and finds someone as "suspicious", they get the "Encore" achievement.

If the Retributionist resurrects a Bodyguard and defends from an attack, they get the "Second Chance" achievement.

 Serial Killers[]

If a Serial Killer is not using their Cautious ability and they are visited by an Tavern Keeper, Bootlegger, Jailor, or Pirate; the player(s) that roleblocked the Serial Killer will be attacked, and if they successfully die, their Last Will will be covered in blood, rendering it deleted.

If a player dies from visiting the Serial Killer and is Cleaned on the same Night, then they will show up as Cleaned in the Graveyard.

If a player dies from visiting the Serial Killer and is Stoned on the same Night, then they will show up as Stoned in the Graveyard.

If a player dies from visiting the Serial Killer and is forged on the same Night, then their role will be replaced as whatever the forgery is. However their Last Will will still be covered in blood.

If the Serial Killer is visited by a Jailor, is not executed, and is not using their Cautious ability; they will get the "Dexter Morgan" achievement.
