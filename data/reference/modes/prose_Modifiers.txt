Modifiers can be used in Custom lobbies, but also appear in the Casual mode decks or in the various All Any decks. These change the game in various ways. Currently, there are eighteen different Modifiers that can be used.

Modifiers here are listed under different unofficial categories, which summarize the differnent ways they impact the game.

Pre-game modifiers do not have any direct effect after the beginning of Day 1.

Information modifiers change the information presented by the game or players during the game.

Gameplay modifiers affect actions that can be taken during the course of the game.

Victory modifiers change the victory requirements for some amount of players in the game.
