Unique Roles are roles that are limited to 1 player per game. Usually, there will never be two in one game, unless an Amnesiac or Amnesiac remembers that role; the Godfather and Mafioso can be inherited, but even for those, only one of each can be alive at once, and even the Archmage cannot Retrain a player into a Coven role that currently or previously existed. For most Unique roles, it may be due to conflicting priority issues, hence only one of them exists.

Traitors in Salem

Currently, there are 18 Unique Roles.

Most Unique Roles have abilities which would cause gameplay issues if there was more than one player with the same role.

For instance, a Jailor jailing another Jailor would cause nonsensical results.

Other Unique Roles have powerful abilities and would upset game balance if there was more than one.

The Veteran and Werewolf are prime examples.

Lastly, some Unique Roles only make practical sense if they are unique.

The Mayor and the Godfather are prime examples.

 Amnesiacs can remember any deceased Unique Role of their choosing. This includes unique Town roles.

Unique Roles primarily consist of support and killing roles.

Four Town roles, two Town Support and Town Killing, all Mafia Killing, two Neutral Killing, three Neutral Chaos, and all Coven roles are unique.

The Plaguebearer and Pestilence, despite functioning as the same faction, are both unique roles.

Seeing two unique roles in a game means that one of the following had happened:

A Forger has forged that player's role. This can happen multiple times.

If the role was a Godfather or Mafioso, a member of the Mafia has died after being promoted. This can happen multiple times with a Mafioso, and usually only once with a Godfather.

An Amnesiac has died after remembering that unique role. This can happen multiple times.

Role uniqueness is usually determined based on the role's alignment: all Town Power, Coven, and Apocalypse roles are unique (except Covenite), while almost every other role is not, with some notable exceptions of Pirate, Executioner, Catalyst, and Vampire.

Seeing two unique roles in a game means that one of the following had happened:

A Enchanter has alterated that player's role. This can happen up to two times, unless an Amnesiac Remembers an Enchanter.

An Illusionist has made their will to appear as that role. This can happen only once, unless an Amnesiac Remembers an Illusionist.

An Amnesiac has died after remembering that unique role. This usually happens only once.

A Vampire-Aligned player was promoted after the previous Vampire died.
