Example of an alignment on a Role Card ( Bodyguard, a Town Protective)

In Town of Salem, there are 12 alignments which serve the purpose of categorizing all of the roles according to their abilities (killing, investigation, protection, support, etc.). The Any role will be a random role of any alignment. The Random Town, Random Mafia, Random Coven or Random Neutral alignments may be a random role of their respective faction.

Trivia[]

In the Public Test Realm, there used to be a now-scrapped Coven Support alignment. Most notably, the only known role to fit in this alignment was the Potion Master.

The Transporter was once mistaken as a Town Protective due to the typo on the Transporter's role card.
