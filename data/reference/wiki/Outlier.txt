
Roles with the Outlier alignment are special. They will only appear in a game when specified in the Role Deck, and often make dramatic changes to the flow of a game. Note that each Outlier role's faction varies.

There are currently six Outlier Roles in the game, with two of them having no abilities or achievements.

 All Outliers[]

The All Outliers modifier allows Outlier Roles to naturally spawn in all "Random Buckets" just like any other alignment. This includes:

 Any, Random Town and Common Town for Town Outlier .

 Any, Random Coven and Common Coven for Coven Outlier .

 Any and Random Neutral for Neutral Outlier .

The following game modes use this modifier: True All Any, Pandemonium

 Town Outlier Roles[]

 Catalyst[]

 Catalyst is a special Town role that can Overcharge a target which allows them to use their ability twice during the following day or night.

 Cursed Soul is immune to Overcharge

 Catalyst is a Unique role.

 Town[]

 Pilgrim is a basic Town role with no abilities.

There may be up to 15 Pilgrims in a game.

 Coven Outlier roles[]

 Cultist[]

The Cultist may convert Townies to the Coven faction during the day, and does not change the target's role.

 Cultist has Basic Defense until they successfully Indoctrinate someone.

 Cultist is a Unique role.

 Coven[]

 Covenite is a basic Coven role that has no abilities and can wield the Necronomicon at Night.

There may be up to 4 Covenites in a game.

 Neutral Outlier roles[]

 Vampire[]

The Vampire can Convert Townies to the Vampire faction, but do not change their target's role.

Players part of the Vampire faction may converse at night, much like the Coven or Apocalypse factions.

If the Vampire dies, a random Converted member of the Vampire faction will change roles and become the new Vampire.

However, there is a certain priority to this promotion mechanic, which goes from: Town Investigative -> Town Protective -> Town Support -> Town Killing -> Town Power -> Town Outlier.

The Vampire has Basic Defense and will not appear Suspicious to a Sheriff if there are no other living members of the Vampire faction.

The Vampire may Drain a player at night, dealing a Basic Attack. If an attempt to Convert a player fails, their target will be attacked instead.

 Vampire is a Unique role, and there may only ever be 1 living Vampire at a time.

 Cursed Soul[]

Players with the Cursed Soul role at the start are part of the Wandering Souls faction.

Their goal is to kill the players with the Cursed Soul role.

The Cursed Soul role trades roles with other players at night, but each player's faction is unchanged.

Members of the Wandering Souls faction may converse at night, much like the Coven or Apocalypse factions.

Members of the Wandering Souls faction have Basic Defense until Day 3.

There may be up to 4 Cursed Souls in a game.

Trivia[]

 Cultist and Vampire are more similar than any other Outliers as both Roles can convert a Townie to their faction.

However, there are some major differences between the Roles, such as:

 Cultist's conversion is a Day ability while Vampire's conversion is a Night ability.

 Cultist's conversion cannot be prevented while Vampire's conversion can. (via Town Protective, Admirer, or Potion Master)

 Cultist only has one charge of Indoctrinate while Vampire has three charges of Convert.

 Pilgrim and Vampire are the only Outliers whose role names do not begin with a C.

Neither Pilgrim nor Covenite have any achievements, causing them to be the only Roles in the game without achievements.

 Covenite is the only Non- Unique Coven role.

 Pilgrim is the only role to be able to spawn more than six of (without the help of an Amnesiac)
